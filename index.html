How version II works

  
    
    
  • From 3 pm to 8:20 pm every hull is flown: it has a top speed and a push and steers for what it is doing.
    • 
    
  • The chapters fix who dies, when, and every shot they describe. The flying brings each hull to those moments.
    • 
    
  • Terra Nova pairs stop, lay on a knot (dashed sightlines), wait for a carrier to hold still, fire, and move.
    • 
    
  • A thin red line from a carrier is her lance coming round. The ship it points at goes sideways.
    • 
    
  • Corner brackets on a carrier mean two ships have her from different angles.
    • 
    
  • Gold dashed ring: longships alongside, boarders on her decks. Gold dots: warriors crossing.
    • 
    
  • Small craft hunt squadrons, run in under carriers, and drop back to Stork Two and Five for KKVs.
    • 
    
  • Transfer arcs to and from Earth are drawn with more bend than a hard burn would really have.
    • 
    
  • Act I knots are under a kilometer across, so zoomed out the hulls fan out round the knot's true place.
    • 
    
  • Act I runs on through Sunday morning: the 3,200 lights out of the great ship, Skua Eleven and then Twelve and Thirteen in among the rows, the Colibrís waiting close in, and Jeb's map for the last half hour.
    • 
    
  • Out there a laser reaches 15 km, so the Skuas work inside the rows. A Colibrí pushes about 7 g on one tank, so she waits about halfway to the Moon and falls in with the lights.
    • 
    
  • The counts, the order and the named places are the page's. Which light went for which city, and whose gun got it, is filled in.
    • 
    
  • On Jeb's map, each thing is drawn over the ground it is above. Coast outline: Natural Earth.
